## Owner proposals under development

- P001 — Accepting educational support creates a minimum one-year service commitment, regardless of graduation. Exact acceptance trigger, accrual, timing, and enforcement remain unresolved.
- P002 — Match service to actual capability; disability alone should not be treated as proof that someone has nothing to contribute. Accommodation, changing capacity, and genuine inability require workable provisions.

## Candidate terms requiring decisions

- P003 — Paid service with normal compensation and benefits; wage parity and living costs must be examined.
- P004 — Additional service proportional to funded education; no ratio beyond the proposed one-year minimum is adopted.
- P005 — Extend eligibility to trades, apprenticeships, technical education, or other advanced training.
- P006 — Accommodation, postponement, transfer, hardship relief, withdrawal terms, and discharge for genuine inability.
- P007 — Specific ownership funds, taxes, borrowing arrangements, benefit design, and governance mechanisms.
- P008 — Use a United States civilian case study as a manageable initial modeling context; this is not an adopted geographic restriction.

- P009 — Contributor decision authority and universal revisability are now established by D005. The v0.2 mechanics for contribution cutoff, seven-day windows, three-participant minimum, independent review, and majority counting are provisional implementation rules open to contributor revision. Technical enforcement, licensing, and distributed maintainer access remain unresolved.

## Priority open questions

1. What minimum security makes meaningful choice possible, and how can it be supplied without intrusive control?
2. Who ultimately pays for each reform, what real capacity is required, and how are vulnerable households protected before costs arrive?
3. Which ownership arrangements broaden access without exposing people to unacceptable risk or creating a new concentration of power?
4. Who can appoint, audit, remove, and challenge the institutions enforcing safeguards?
5. When exactly does an education-service obligation begin, and what happens when the provider or government fails?
6. What service compensation, duration, placement choices, accommodations, and exit terms preserve practical freedom?
7. Which evidence would warrant expansion, revision, or rejection of each candidate mechanism?
