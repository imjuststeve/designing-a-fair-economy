Below three, ordinary decisions retain strict majority and need at least one participant; governance still uses two-thirds. Ordinary review and voting each last seven days; governance and purpose review and voting each last fourteen. Freeze eligibility and exact text, separate unrelated decisions, restart for material changes, preserve dissent, and integrate approved text without a second policy veto. These operating details make the adopted recommendation actionable. Everything remains revisable. This is a founding policy adoption, not a community ballot or an ongoing unilateral founder power.
