# Current requirements and open questions

Current edition: September 27, 2026.

## Framework requirements

The framework shall connect economic security, meaningful individual freedom, diverse ambitions and outcomes, broader productive ownership, contestable power, and a survivable transition. Mechanisms remain subject to evidence, feasibility analysis, and contributor review. Current governance and documentation requirements are specified in the project policies [P1]-[P3].

## Education-service working design

- Regional median compensation for comparable work, adjusted for experience, qualifications, duties, hours, ordinary pay schedules and benefits, and documented hard-to-fill conditions. Education funding does not justify discounted wages.
- Like-for-like service in return for educational support. A minimum one-year commitment regardless of graduation is proposed; exact accrual and proportionality require definition.
- Temporary outside employment does not discharge service. Suitable qualifying placement offered before the six-month deadline takes priority.
- Interim placement in government agencies or managed systems is a candidate route. Unrelated interim work does not fully discharge the obligation.
- No automatic six-month discharge. Maximum waiting time, the milestone's final function, and government-failure remedies require definition.
- Capability-based matching and accommodation. Education participation does not establish sustained capacity for every employment role.

## Requirements for improvement

1. Define the economic floor, household effects, financing, real resource needs, and safeguards against intrusive conditions.
2. Compare ownership and market arrangements, including access, control, risk, liquidity, and wealth concentration.
3. Map institutional appointment, audit, removal, appeal, and capture risks.
4. Define the education-service acceptance trigger, benefit scope, vocational eligibility, service equivalence, duration, compensation benchmarks, and exit terms.
5. Define placement suitability, transition notice, geographical requirements, interim credit, niche-field treatment, and government obligations.
6. Evaluate actual employment as a contribution criterion, waiting-period extensions, reasonable exceptions, privacy-preserving verification, and appeals. No final good-faith test is established.
7. Compare related placements, prospective service-pathway assessment, voluntary funded retraining, bounded waiting, and government-breach remedies. These are candidate options.
8. Appraise every factual claim against supporting and conflicting evidence; resolve the source limitations in [P4].
9. Develop reproducible fiscal, household, and workforce models and explicit evaluation and stopping criteria.
10. Resolve licensing, technical governance enforcement, and distributed maintenance arrangements.

## Publication requirements

All current documents shall use formal scholarly presentation, nearby numbered citations, and an end-of-document reference list. Separate findings, proposals, assumptions, and uncertainty. Exclude internal conversations, superseded policy narratives, and accounts of revision decisions. State the analytical justification for current requirements and the work needed to resolve gaps [P3].
